After the event

An event ends and the guest list goes quiet. This page asks one question of IslandDAO v5: did the builders who checked in keep using stablecoins after their first day?

The answer

Before and after the first check-in

A payment is a stablecoin transfer between a builder's wallet and a wallet outside the guest list. Sent and received are counted apart: a prize or a refund is a payment received. Swaps, DeFi deposits, transfers between two guests and dust are not payments. After the first check-in, not because of it.

Week by week

Builders with a payment or a swap, by week

Weeks are counted from each builder's own first check-in, so week +1 is a different calendar week for each builder.

By time at the event

Does staying longer go with paying more?

Share of builders who sent at least one payment. "Under 5" means fewer than 5 builders, zero included.

How it is made

From a guest list to an answer

Guest listOne row per guest: a wallet and a check-in time. Here it is IslandDAO's on-chain check-ins, put in the shape of a Luma export.
ReadGecko reads each wallet's stablecoin history from Solana. This step is free.
CleanEach transaction is filed as a payment sent or received, a swap, a program move, a transfer between guests or dust.
CompareFor each guest: the 14 days before the first check-in against the 14 days after.
Roll upOnly counts leave the dataset. Every value keeps its source and read time.
  • Counts only. No wallet addresses, no names, no amounts.
  • Every count is rounded to the nearest 5, totals included. A count under 5 shows as "under 5". Percentages come from the rounded counts.
  • Numbers count check-in identifiers, one per account, not verified people.
  • Stablecoins on Solana only: USDC, USDT, PYUSD and USDG. Other tokens and other chains are not read.
  • Check-in days are local days in Florianópolis. Activity days are UTC days.
  • The 14 days after include days spent at the event itself. Most builders stayed 4 days or more.
  • There is no comparison group yet. A rise can come from the calendar or from who chose to attend.
  • A wallet made for the event has no history before it. That lowers the "before" numbers.

Your Luma file

What your event needs to get the same answer

Three settings in Luma, switched on before registration opens:

  • Ask for a Solana wallet. Luma has a wallet field in the registration form, and the guest proves they own the wallet.
  • Check guests in. The check-in time is day 0 for each guest.
  • Tag your invite links. A tag on each partner's link shows later which partner brought the guests who stayed active.
